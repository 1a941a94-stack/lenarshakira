[SIZE=5][B]Projects that are a good fit[/B][/SIZE]

[B]Manual workflow → internal system[/B]
Your team currently works through spreadsheets, messages and repeated manual steps. You need one structured system with users, roles, tasks, statuses and reports.

[B]Idea → SaaS MVP[/B]
You have a product idea and need accounts, subscriptions, payments, dashboards and an admin panel — not just a landing page.

[B]Disconnected tools → automation[/B]
Several services need to exchange data automatically through APIs, webhooks or scheduled workflows.

[B]Marketplace → complete platform[/B]
You need buyers, sellers, listings, commissions, orders, moderation and operations in one product.

[B]Existing product → next version[/B]
The project already works, but you need new modules, a better admin panel, another provider, AI functionality or a cleaner workflow.

[SIZE=5][B]How I work[/B][/SIZE]

You do not need a 50-page technical specification.

A useful first message contains:
1. What you want to build
2. Who will use it
3. The most important functions
4. Required integrations
5. Your approximate deadline
